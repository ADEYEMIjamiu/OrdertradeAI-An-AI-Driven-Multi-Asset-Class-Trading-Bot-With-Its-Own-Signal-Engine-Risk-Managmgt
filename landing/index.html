FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The questions that come up before connecting a broker.

Is this financial advice?

No. OrderTrade AI shows you graded signals and the reasoning behind them, and you decide whether to act on any of it. It isn't a registered investment adviser, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any specific security, coin, or instrument. Trading involves risk, including of loss.

Can I trade with real money, or is it demo only?

Every account starts in demo mode, with no real money at risk. When you're ready, you can enable live trading from your account settings: read the risk disclosure, confirm you understand a bug or broker outage could cause a real loss, and opt in broker by broker. It's a deliberate decision you make, not something that happens by default.

Which brokers do I need?

Alpaca for US stocks, Binance, Kraken, or Luno for crypto, and eToro or MT4/5 for forex, commodities, and indices. Alpaca, Binance, eToro, and MT4/5 start in demo or paper mode, with live trading available once you opt in; Kraken and Luno connect straight to your real account instead, since neither offers a demo. Connect whichever ones match the asset classes you want signals for. You don't need all six, and you can add more later from your dashboard.

Do you ever hold or touch my money?

No. Every trade is placed directly on your own connected broker account using your own API keys, which are encrypted at rest. OrderTrade AI is a signal and execution layer, not a custodian; see "Your money stays in your own broker account" above.

What happens after the 14-day free trial?

If you don't cancel before the trial ends, billing continues at $39/month through Stripe. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. There's no long-term contract, and canceling during the trial means you're never charged.
