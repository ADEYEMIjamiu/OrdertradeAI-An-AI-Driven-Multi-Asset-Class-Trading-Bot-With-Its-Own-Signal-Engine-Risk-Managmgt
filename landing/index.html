How it works

Three steps between connecting an account and reviewing your first signal.

01

Connect your own broker

Link Alpaca, Binance or eToro with your own API keys. They are encrypted at rest and only ever decrypted to act on your own account. OrderTrade AI never pools or holds anyone's funds.

02

The AI scores opportunities

The signal engine reads market data across your enabled asset classes and produces a graded, ranked list, sized against your real connected-account balance.

03

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The questions that come up before connecting a broker.

Is this financial advice?

No. OrderTrade AI shows you graded signals and the reasoning behind them -- you decide whether to act on any of it. It isn't a registered investment adviser, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any specific security, coin, or instrument. Trading involves risk, including of loss.

Why is it limited to paper and demo trading right now?

Early access is deliberately paper/demo-only across every connected broker -- there is no control anywhere in the product that switches this on for live trading. It lets you see exactly how the signal engine and risk management behave with your real account balance and settings, without real money on the line, while the product is still new.

Which brokers do I need?

Alpaca for US stocks (paper), Binance for crypto (testnet), and eToro for forex and commodities (demo). Connect whichever ones match the asset classes you want signals for -- you don't need all three, and you can add more later from your dashboard.

Do you ever hold or touch my money?

No. Every trade is placed directly on your own connected broker account using your own API keys, which are encrypted at rest. OrderTrade AI is a signal and execution layer, not a custodian -- see "Your money stays in your own broker account" above.

What happens after the 7-day free trial?

If you don't cancel before the trial ends, billing continues at $39/month through Stripe. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings -- no long-term contract, and canceling during the trial means you're never charged.
