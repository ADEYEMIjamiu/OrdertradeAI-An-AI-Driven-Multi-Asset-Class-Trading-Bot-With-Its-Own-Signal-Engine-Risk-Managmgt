How it works

Three steps between connecting an account and reviewing your first signal.

01

Connect your own broker

Link Alpaca, Binance or eToro with your own API keys. They are encrypted at rest and only ever decrypted to act on your own account. OrderTrade AI never pools or holds anyone's funds.

02

The AI scores opportunities

The signal engine reads market data across your enabled asset classes and produces a graded, ranked list, sized against your real connected-account balance.

03

You review, then execute

Preview shows exactly what would be bought and why. Nothing is placed until you check the confirmation box and click Execute yourself.

Risk management runs in the background

Protection that applies once a position is open, not just at entry.

Stop-loss and take-profit

Set from real price structure at trade time, not a fixed percentage guess.

Trailing-profit lock and break-even ratchet

Gives back less of an open gain as a position moves in your favor, with partial profit-taking along the way.

Hard time-based exit

Positions held past a set number of days are closed regardless of current profit or loss.

Portfolio-level exposure cap

A ceiling on how much of your account can be at risk at once, checked before every new trade.

Kill switch

One control stops all automated activity across every connected broker immediately.
